# AutoMaktab

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

AutoMaktab is a driving-school management platform for owners, branch managers, registrars and accountants in Uzbekistan. The public site helps a director understand how students, tuition, debt, schedules, attendance and branches can be managed together, then open the synthetic demo or request a trial.

## Operating context

School work is often spread across Telegram groups, Excel sheets and paper records. The requested animation explains bringing these workflows into one platform. It is a visual explanation, not a claim that messages, files or paper are automatically imported.

## Capabilities and constraints

The public marketing site lives here; the authenticated CRM is a separate application at app.automaktab.uz. Keep Uzbek, Russian and English routes, localized metadata, accessible navigation, demo tracking, trial form validation and request handling. A demo uses sample data. A trial uses the school's data for 30 days with terms agreed in writing; it is not advertised as free. Import terms and GPS connectivity are not presumed.

Daily opt-in Telegram reports contain yesterday's revenue and new student count at 08:00 Tashkent time: company totals for the owner and the manager's own branch. The landing's three-branch breakdown is explicitly an illustrative preview; it is not advertised as the current Telegram payload. Its aggregate is computed from numeric sample branch rows.

Verified public examples show lead stages, assignee, source, follow-up and conversion; vehicle assignment, maintenance, insurance documents and fuel records; theory/practice schedules, attendance and configurable internal school tests. Internal tests are distinct from state exams. State sync and bank integration remain planned.

## Brand commitments

Use automaktab.uz and the brand palette from Notion's “02 — Vizual identika”. The user requested Duolingo-inspired approachable typography, rounded controls and tactile interaction, plus a Pluto-inspired consolidation animation. Public product demonstrations use authored HTML/SVG motion scenes with localized synthetic-data labels. Do not render CRM screenshots or invent customer statistics, testimonials or working integrations. Existing screenshot assets remain available but unused by the landing page.
