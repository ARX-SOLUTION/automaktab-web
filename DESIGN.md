# DESIGN.md: automaktab-web visual design system

> **Decision status (2026-09-26):** Approved. Liquid glass is the public visual baseline. [ADR 0004](./docs/adr/0004-liquid-glass-public-visual-system.md) supersedes the Nazorat markazi canvas and the glass / hero-WebGL ban in [ADR 0001](./docs/adr/0001-public-brand-and-control-center-direction.md). Brand, story, proof, conversion, and claim limits from ADR 0001 still apply.

## Resolved replacement brief

- Direction: **liquid glass**. The page is a mineral-ink field, one liquid hero, and glass surfaces for navigation, proof, cards, and forms. There is one composition and no public theme toggle. Purple gradients, generic frosted-white SaaS cards, and corporate-blue templates are outside this system.
- Brand: the `automaktab.uz` wordmark plus Signal Lime as the control accent. `AutoDrive`, car, wheel, road, and traffic-light motifs stay absent. Logo master SVGs keep their exported paths. A new logo system is not part of this change.
- Palette: Field Ink `#08140F`; Glass Ink `#102018` (opaque fallback); Paper `#F3F6EE`; Signal Lime `#C6FF3D`; On Lime `#102008`; Muted `#C5D1C2`; Danger `#FFAD9E`. Paper on Field is about 15:1. Muted on Field is about 12:1. On Lime on Signal Lime stays above 7:1. Lime is the primary control, not body text on a light fill. Logo system blue is not a marketing accent.
- Surfaces: tokens `--glass-fill`, `--glass-border`, `--glass-blur`, and the tiers `nav`, `panel`, `proof`, `form`, `inset`. Each tier is a dark translucent fill, a 1px refraction border (paper highlight into a lime edge), and `backdrop-filter` where the surface is the outer shell. Proof bezels keep the screenshot opaque. If `backdrop-filter` is missing, the fill becomes Glass Ink at 92% opacity. Do not stack blurred glass inside blurred glass.
- Typography: `Outfit` for the Latin wordmark and short display lines; `Manrope` for body, UI, CTA, Cyrillic, and localized reading. Logo files remain outlined paths and do not require a webfont. Body is at least 16px with a 1.5–1.75 line height and a controlled reading measure.
- Story: operational disorder, then the control surface, then three product proofs, then the immediate demo, then objections.
- Proof: real captures from the synthetic demo account. Dashboard, Schedule, and Attendance stay the proof set. Cropping for composition is allowed. Redrawing or changing product state is not.
- Conversion: `Demo’ni hozir oching` is the sole primary action and stays a solid lime control. Its click opens the demo-intent route and establishes the synthetic demo session. Link prefetch stays off, credentials stay out of the URL, and a failed automatic attempt shows a manual retry. `15 daqiqalik tanishuv` is the secondary glass-shell action. The form requires name, telephone, and region. School name and student count stay optional. Labels stay visible, errors sit beside their fields, and Telegram is offered only after success.
- Trust: verified product behavior and the confirmed 30-day trial only.
- SEO: the H1 carries the human promise. Title, description, and supporting copy keep the localized equivalents of `avtomaktab boshqaruv tizimi` and `avtomaktab CRM`. Search text is semantic HTML.
- Motion: the liquid hero is the atmosphere. GSAP provides two signature moments. The hero settles the statement, the signal action, and the first proof with transform only, so the text and primary action are visible on first paint. One desktop pinned disorder stack uses ScrollTrigger. Mobile stays an ordinary vertical sequence. Use scoped `useGSAP`, `gsap.matchMedia`, transform, interruptible timelines, and cleanup on unmount. No scroll-jacking, smooth-scroll replacement, custom cursor, body-copy parallax, or a second pinned section. Reduced motion and no-JS render the complete static story, including the hero gradient.
- Hero liquid: one self-contained WebGL canvas on the landing hero. Idle-load it, cap device pixel ratio, draw at a reduced internal resolution, pause when the hero is off-screen or the tab is hidden, and drop the context on unmount. Skip it for `prefers-reduced-motion` and save-data. The static gradient underneath is the fallback and the no-JS view. The canvas is `aria-hidden` and is not the LCP element.
- Performance: no background video and no second 3D scene. Reserve screenshot dimensions, use responsive AVIF/WebP, prioritize only the LCP image, and lazy-load lower proofs. Targets: LCP ≤2.5s, INP ≤200ms, and CLS ≤0.1 at the 75th percentile.
- Interaction: every target is at least 44×44px, focus remains a visible lime outline, color is never the only state, and the layout is checked at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440px without horizontal scrolling.
