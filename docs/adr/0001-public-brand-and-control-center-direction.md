# Use automaktab.uz and the Control Center direction

The public brand is `automaktab.uz`, described as “Avtomaktablar uchun boshqaruv tizimi”; `AutoDrive` is not used on the public marketing surface. The existing “Road Signal” road, car, and amber-signal identity is superseded by a “Nazorat markazi” direction: soft editorial light surfaces, deep ink product zones, an electric-green control point, and real screenshots from a synthetic demo account. Schoolify remains a content-and-evidence benchmark, not a visual template, because another dark dashboard-led ERP landing would not differentiate the product.

The page tells one story: operational disorder becomes a visible control center, three real product views prove the claim, the visitor can enter the demo immediately, and the final section resolves objections. The initial proof set is Dashboard, Schedule, and Attendance; Finance crops may be reused in the first SEO article. The site uses one curated light composition with fixed dark product zones, not a user-facing theme switcher. This wave introduces a strong `automaktab.uz` wordmark with an electric-green point; a full logo system remains separate.

The proof set was verified against the live synthetic demo on 2026-08-31. Dashboard exposes the owner revenue/debt overview, Schedule shows a populated weekly theory/practice calendar, and a completed Attendance lesson opens a ten-student roster with present/late/absent/excused summaries. The visible names and staff labels originate from `autodrive-backend/prisma/seed-demo.ts`, not customer data. Final marketing assets may crop these views for composition, but must not redraw or alter the product state.

The visual system is precision-editorial operations, not generic SaaS brutalism: Control Ink `#0B1720`, Field Paper `#F5F7F2`, white evidence surfaces, Signal Lime `#C6FF3D`, Data Blue `#2F6BFF`, and muted text `#52606D`. Lime is used with ink for the primary action and active control signal, never as text on the light field. Blue is reserved for links and product data, not as a competing marketing accent. `Unbounded` is limited to the wordmark and short display statements; `Manrope` carries all reading and interface text.

The primary action is the immediate synthetic-data demo. A short on-site introduction form is secondary and may offer Telegram after a successful submission. Public claims are limited to verified product behavior and the confirmed 30-day trial. Response-time, one-day setup, 24/7 support, daily-backup, unlimited-branch, YHXBB-integration, and conflict-prevention claims remain excluded until there is evidence for them.

The public blog currently contains one published three-locale article. Its Excel/daftar framing is useful, but its double-booking prevention, regulatory/YHXBB compliance, and unlimited-branch claims are not supported by the audited product. The article must be corrected through the existing content workflow before the new blog route makes it indexable; the marketing frontend must not hide or rewrite selected paragraphs at render time.

This decision applies to `automaktab-web` public branding. Renaming internal repositories, packages, APIs, or source identifiers is a separate change and is not implied by this decision.
