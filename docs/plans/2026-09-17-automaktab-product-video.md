# Automaktab product video production approach

## Objective

Create a product-led campaign video for Uzbek driving-school owners that moves from operational disorder to a visible control center and sends viewers to the live synthetic demo.

Primary CTA: `Demo’ni hozir oching — automaktab.uz`.

## Scope

- Primary master: 30-second vertical `1080x1920` video for Telegram, Reels, and Stories.
- Adaptations: 15-second vertical cut and 30-second `1920x1080` horizontal cut.
- Uzbek narration with burned subtitles plus a clean master without subtitles.
- Product-led, faceless treatment; no presenter, fake customer, car, road, or traffic-light cliché.
- Do not publish externally without a separate final approval.

## Settled creative decisions

- Hook: `Rahbar yo‘q paytda nazorat qayerda qoladi?`
- Visual ratio: approximately 20% generative atmosphere and 80% verified product evidence.
- Voice: natural, confident Uzbek male voice, approximately 30–40 in perceived age; calm and direct, not an announcer.
- Sound: restrained electronic pulse with subtle operational sounds and one clean signal when the lime control point activates.
- End card: one CTA only; Telegram details belong in the post caption, not the video.
- Opening: editorial fragments representing spreadsheet, notebook, and calendar on Field Paper; no generated text or customer data.
- Logo motion: flat Folded Ascent ribbon draw/fold followed by one Signal Lime pulse; no 3D morph, bevel, or added shadow.
- Product proof: real screen recordings from the synthetic demo; approved screenshots are the fallback and may only be cropped, not redrawn.

## Production checkpoints

1. Storyboard stills and locked narration layout.
2. A 6–8 second motion test covering the opening transition, logo activation, and first product reveal.
3. Complete 30-second review draft.
4. Final masters and adaptations after approval.

## Brand and evidence guardrails

- Use Control Ink `#0B1720`, Field Paper `#F5F7F2`, Data Blue `#2F6BFF`, Deep Data Blue `#174BC5`, and Signal Lime `#C6FF3D` only.
- Use Unbounded only for the wordmark and short display lines; use Manrope for readable copy.
- Preserve the logo geometry and the single lime control point.
- Never fabricate, redraw, or cosmetically alter product state.
- Use synthetic-demo data only; no customer PII.
- Keep text to one short statement per shot, inside vertical safe areas.
- Claims are limited to verified current product behavior and the confirmed 30-day trial.
- Music and voice assets require commercial-use clearance before final delivery.

## Execution constraint

If it remains unavailable, stop at the approved storyboard/motion specification rather than substituting an off-brand renderer without approval.

## Approval boundary
